Network & connection classification

Provider-reported indicators

Yes / No reflects this provider’s current response, not a guarantee. Unknown means no result was provided. Risk flags and scores are not proof of abuse; VPN and Tor use alone do not imply wrongdoing.

Observed Internet routing

A network range is not a website. The slash number is a CIDR prefix length, not a web-page path. Routing network IDs (ASNs) identify networks announcing the range. These are observations from routing collectors. They do not reveal a device’s local network, identify its owner, or guarantee global reachability.

Public registration · RDAP

Published registry contacts

These are registration or network-operation contacts, not the device owner’s account or personal identity. Redacted contacts may be absent.

Published abuse-reporting contact

A contact for reporting network abuse; it does not mean this IP has committed abuse.

Reputation research

Detailed report histories usually need a provider account or API plan. The app does not claim a clean reputation when those reports have not been checked. These links open the selected IP on external services.

All returned provider fields · JSON

Unmodified successful provider responses are included here. Their fields may be missing, stale, or inconsistent. Geolocation responses may include postal estimates, timezone offsets, country context, and additional network fields.


      
What an IP cannot disclose
Device model, operating system, Windows username, signed-in accounts, email, MAC address, passwords, files, and exact street address are not available from this public IP lookup. Devices behind a router can share one IP. Authenticated device management or information voluntarily shared by the device is required for device-specific details.
